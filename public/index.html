🛠️ Quick Event Setup Guide

  1. Connect this PC to the LED screen via HDMI and click "OPEN TV DISPLAY" (press F11 for fullscreen).
  2. Connect the phone to the same Wi-Fi or this PC's Mobile Hotspot.
  3. Make sure the network adapter dropdown above has your Wi-Fi / Hotspot IP selected.
  4. Open the camera on the phone and scan the QR code above.
  5. Slot the phone into the wooden slingshot casing.
  6. Have the guest take a selfie, pick a paint color, and tap "LOCK & LOAD". A live red laser dot will track where the slingshot is pointing on the LED screen wall! (You can also press 'L' on the TV display to test the red dot anytime).
  7. Pull & release the rubber band — the accelerometer detects the snap shockwave and instantly splashes the paintball on the LED screen!
